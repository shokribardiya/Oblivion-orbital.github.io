OBLIVION / INDEPENDENT TECHNOLOGY COLLECTIVE

Engineering systems for complex information.

Oblivion is an independent technology collective focused on software engineering, artificial intelligence, data systems, cybersecurity research, automation, and computational systems.

EXPLORE OBLIVION VIEW PROJECTS

Complexity is not the problem.

Modern information is fragmented across systems, datasets, documents, networks, and machines.

The challenge is not simply collecting information. The challenge is understanding how information connects.

Oblivion develops software systems designed to structure, connect, analyze, and visualize complex information.

HOW WE THINK

The Oblivion approach

Five principles that guide every system we build.
01

First Principles

Understand the underlying system before building abstractions around it.

02

Structure Over Noise

Raw information becomes useful when it can be organized and interpreted.

03

Connections Matter

The relationship between pieces of information can be as important as the information itself.

04

Security by Design

Security belongs in the architecture, not as an afterthought.

05

Experimentation

Ideas should be tested in real systems rather than accepted because they are conventional.
